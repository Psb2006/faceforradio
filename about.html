About the show

Fair warning: this page has a lot of bass in it.

Hi, I'm Patrick

I'm a first-year Geography student at Christ's College, which — if you're wondering — has approximately nothing to do with why I have a radio show. That's just the bass. I've been playing since I was six, which means I've now spent more of my life playing bass than not, and somewhere along the way I turned into the person at parties who won't stop talking about session musicians nobody else has heard of.

I'm from Liverpool originally, which I will absolutely bring up at every opportunity, and I ended up at CamFM because I wanted an excuse to make other people listen to my bass rambling on purpose, once a week, with a microphone, instead of just cornering people at parties.

A Face For Radio is a weekly music show — live every Tuesday, 9–10am on CamFM 97.2 — built around the stuff I actually care about: brilliant bass players who don't get enough credit, live performances that hit different to the studio version, and whatever daft or dramatic thing has happened in the music world that week.

What to expect week to week

The running order moves around depending on what I'm into that week, but most episodes cover:

Segment one

Low End Legend of the Week

One bass player, their career, and a track that shows exactly why they matter. This is the segment I will die on a hill for.

Segment two

Live Music Education

A live recording that shows a band or artist's rawness and feel outside the studio — the mistakes, the energy, the bits a polished record smooths over.

Segment three

The News

Three quick headlines from the music world that week — chart news, reunions, industry drama, gear stories — delivered with far more enthusiasm than the actual news deserves. I post the same three headlines on the Music News page after each show.

Segment four

Back to Back Bangers

Two songs, no chat in between. A rare chance for you to hear from someone other than me.

Every now and then I'll also run Guess the Song — a "stem splitter" game where I build a track up from just the drums and you guess it live in the CamFM chat — and occasionally a themed special: an artist retrospective, a city's music scene, or an interview if I can rope someone interesting into it.
