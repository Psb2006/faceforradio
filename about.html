The bit where I explain myself

About the show

Fair warning: I will bring up bass at some point. It's not the whole show, I promise.

Hi, I'm Patrick

I'm a first-year Geography student at Christ's College, which has approximately nothing to do with why I have a radio show — that's just a lifelong love of music. I've been playing bass since I was six, which probably explains why I somehow turned into the person at parties who won't stop talking about session musicians nobody else has heard of.

I'm from Liverpool originally, which I will absolutely bring up at every opportunity, and I ended up at CamFM because I wanted an excuse to make other people listen to my music rambling on purpose, once a week, with a microphone, instead of just cornering people at parties.

A Face For Radio is a weekly music show — live every Tuesday, 9–10am on CamFM 97.2 — built around whatever I'm into that week: new tracks, old favourites, live performances that hit different to the studio version, whatever daft or dramatic thing has happened in the music world lately, and yes, every so often, a bass player who deserves way more credit than they get.

What to expect week to week

The running order moves around depending on what I'm into that week, but most episodes cover:

Segment one

Low End Legend of the Week

One bass player, their career, and a track that shows exactly why they matter. This is the segment I will die on a hill for.

Segment two

Live Music Education

A live recording that shows a band or artist's rawness and feel outside the studio — the mistakes, the energy, the bits a polished record smooths over.

Segment three

The News

Three quick headlines from the music world that week — chart news, reunions, industry drama, gear stories — delivered with far more enthusiasm than the actual news deserves. I post the same three headlines on the homepage after each show.

Segment four

Back to Back Bangers

Two songs, no chat in between. A rare chance for you to hear from someone other than me.

Every now and then I'll also run Guess the Song — a "stem splitter" game where I build a track up from just the drums and you guess it live in the CamFM chat — and occasionally a themed special: an artist retrospective, a city's music scene, or an interview if I can rope someone interesting into it.
