A Face For Radio

A weekly radio show, loosely based around my love for music — new tracks, old favourites, the odd live gem, and the week's music news read out like it's breaking. (And yes, I'll always find a way to ramble about bass players)

What's actually in the show

Every episode moves around a bit, but a lot of the time it usually looks something like this:

Segment one

Low End Legend of the Week

A deep dive into one bass player — who they are, what they've played on, and one song that proves why they deserve your attention.

Segment two

Live Music Education

A live recording, picked to show a band or artist stripped of studio polish — all the rawness and feel you don't always get on the record.

Segment three

The News

Three quick music headlines from the week — chart news, reunions, gear drama, industry gossip, etc. Catch up on it here.

Segment four

Back to Back Bangers

Even I like to shut up for a bit... sometimes. Two brilliant songs that have caught my eye in the week with minimal chat.

We like to change the show structure whenever we can so expect surprises!
